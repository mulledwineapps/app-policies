# Plant Care Tracker Privacy Policy

Last updated November 11, 2026.

This policy describes how Plant Care Tracker, developed by Mulledwineapps, handles data. You can use the app without an account.

## Plant records and photos

The app stores your plant records, tags, photos, and related details such as capture dates on your device. The app uses these data to manage your collection and display your gallery and archive. The app does not send the contents of your collection to the developer or to analytics services.

The app uses the camera when you choose to take plant photos. When you import photos, the app reads the files you select and may use the capture date from their metadata. If you export a photo or a backup, the file is saved to the location you choose. On older Android versions, saving a photo to the system gallery may require permission to write to shared storage.

If you save files to a cloud storage service or use system backups, the provider or platform may process those data under its own policies. On Android, the system backup may include app data depending on your device settings.

## Android analytics and crash reports

The Android release may include the following services:

- **AppMetrica (Yandex)** receives information about app launches and sessions, app version, device model, OS version, technical identifiers, and IP address. The service may infer your country from your IP address. If you submit a rating within the app, the service receives the rating value. These data help the developer understand how the app works and how people use it. [AppMetrica data security documentation](https://appmetrica.yandex.com/docs/en/new-users/data-security)
- **VK Tracer** receives reports about crashes and freezes. A report may include the error message, stack trace, app version, device model, and OS version. The developer uses these reports to diagnose and fix problems. [Tracer crash reporting documentation](https://apptracer.ru/doc/intro/crashes/)

## Retention and deletion

Your collection stays in the app's local storage until you delete it. You can delete individual records and photos in the app. On Android, you can remove the local collection by clearing the app's data in device settings. Delete exported photos and backups separately from wherever you saved them.

Analytics retention depends on AppMetrica's settings and policies. VK Tracer retains crash events for the previous 90 days. Deleting your local collection does not remove technical data already sent to these services. For questions about accessing or deleting those data, contact the developer using the address below.
